# Design principles — The Summit Collections

These rules govern the selected Dark Gallery flagship.

1. Let founder-supplied garment photography lead; do not decorate over product detail.
2. Use catalogue references (SC-001–SC-014) until the founder approves official product names.
3. Keep one coherent typographic pairing: expressive editorial serif for display, quiet sans-serif for navigation and utility.
4. Align every element to an intentional grid; use asymmetry only when it improves image composition.
5. Use a restrained warm-charcoal and mineral-neutral palette; avoid default black-and-gold luxury styling.
6. Prefer considered image scale and generous negative space over rounded cards, gradients and ornamental UI.
7. Product images keep their natural proportions; use per-image fit and positioning metadata and never crop important garment details.
8. Motion must communicate hierarchy, reveal, continuity or feedback; if its purpose cannot be explained, remove it.
9. Animate transform and opacity; avoid layout-affecting animation and blanket `transition: all`.
10. Use short, interruptible ease-out transitions; avoid bounce, autoplay spectacle and scroll hijacking.
11. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all content and controls remain immediately available in reduced-motion mode.
12. Mobile is a first-class composition, not a compressed desktop layout; preserve legibility, image clarity and 44px touch targets.
13. Every action has one clear label and one clear destination; keep one persistent WhatsApp entry.
14. Design empty, error, focus, hover, selected and open states—not only the happy path.
15. Never invent product names, prices, sizes, availability, testimonials, awards, delivery promises or brand history.

## Selected direction

**B — Dark Gallery** was selected by the user on 26 November 2026. The full storefront uses warm charcoal, gallery-like product framing, editorial serif typography, a horizontal lookbook strip and a four-column desktop / two-column mobile catalogue. Motion is deliberately restrained; no Three.js, WebGL, scroll hijacking or heavy motion library is used.

## Reference standards consulted

- [Vercel Web Interface Guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines): semantic HTML, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, clear labels, image loading and performance discipline.
- [Vercel Agent Skills — web-design-guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/web-design-guidelines): accessibility, forms, animation, typography, image and performance review checklist.
- [Taste Skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill): original art direction, one coherent system, real subject photography, quiet micro-interactions and browser-based critique rather than generic page templates.

These are implementation references, not copied templates. The brand's actual product photographs and existing enquiry flow remain the source of truth.
