# Design principles — The Summit Collections

These rules govern the flagship redesign and both direction prototypes.

1. Let founder-supplied garment photography lead; do not decorate over product detail.
2. Use catalogue references (SC-001–SC-014) until the founder approves official product names.
3. Keep one coherent typographic pairing: expressive editorial serif for display, quiet sans-serif for navigation and utility.
4. Align every element to an intentional grid; use asymmetry only when it improves image composition.
5. Use a restrained palette sampled from the actual garments and photographs; avoid default black-and-gold luxury styling.
6. Prefer hairline rules, generous whitespace and considered image scale over rounded cards, gradients and ornamental UI.
7. Product images keep their natural proportions; use explicit per-image positioning and never crop important garment details.
8. Motion must communicate hierarchy, reveal, continuity or feedback; if its purpose cannot be explained, remove it.
9. Animate transform and opacity; avoid layout-affecting animation and blanket `transition: all`.
10. Use short, interruptible ease-out transitions; avoid bounce, autoplay spectacle and scroll hijacking.
11. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all content and controls remain immediately available in reduced-motion mode.
12. Mobile is a first-class composition, not a compressed desktop layout; preserve legibility, image clarity and 44px touch targets.
13. Every action has one clear label and one clear destination; remove duplicate WhatsApp calls to action.
14. Design empty, error, focus, hover, selected and open states—not only the happy path.
15. Never invent product names, prices, sizes, availability, testimonials, awards, delivery promises or brand history.

## Direction prototypes

- A — Atelier Index: warm paper, editorial index, asymmetrical product photography, fine rules and quiet motion.
- B — Dark Gallery: warm charcoal, gallery-lit imagery, strong typographic contrast and a horizontal editorial rhythm.
