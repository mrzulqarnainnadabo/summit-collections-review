# Selected direction — Dark Gallery

**Decision:** Direction B, selected by the user on 26 November 2026.

1. The deeper warm-neutral ground gives the founder-supplied garments a gallery context without defaulting to black-and-gold luxury clichés.
2. The restrained serif/sans pairing gives the brand an editorial voice while keeping navigation, catalogue references and enquiry details practical.
3. Full-garment image presentation takes priority over decorative cropping; product frames use `contain` and per-item positioning metadata.
4. The horizontal lookbook strip adds a tactile editorial pause; the catalogue grid remains the primary way to browse and enquire.
5. Motion is limited to reveal, image hover and short interface transitions, with reduced-motion support and no scroll hijacking.

The complete storefront now uses Direction B as its foundation. The Atelier Index prototype is removed from the selected branch. The Dark Gallery prototype remains as a reference route.

**Open approval items:** official product names, current prices (including reconfirmation of the legacy Yasmine price), sizes, availability, delivery terms and provisional colour groupings remain founder-confirmation items.
