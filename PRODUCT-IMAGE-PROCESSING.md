# Product image processing record

Date: 23 November 2026

The original founder-supplied screenshots are preserved unchanged at the repository root. Product-facing WebP assets in `product-images/` were created with Pillow using deterministic rectangular crops of the central post-media region. The crop trims the Instagram account header and the lower engagement/caption region; it does not alter garment pixels through generative editing, colour grading, sharpening, background replacement, or synthetic reconstruction.

All assets are normalised to 828 × 1035 (4:5). The 4:5 format supports consistent product cards and detail views. Product images are local assets, not remote Instagram screenshots loaded by the website.

Limitations: Instagram UI elements embedded inside the media itself, background objects inside the original photograph, compression artefacts, and low-resolution garment details cannot be reliably removed or restored by this process. These remain candidates for a future controlled product shoot or manual pixel-level retouching. Do not describe these crops as fully restored studio photography.
